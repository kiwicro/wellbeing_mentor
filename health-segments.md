# Health & Wellbeing — Segments

A comprehensive breakdown of segments covering weight loss, healthy eating, exercise, and overall physical and mental health.

## Physical Body — Inputs

1. **Nutrition** — macronutrients (protein/carbs/fat), micronutrients (vitamins/minerals), fiber, calorie balance, meal timing, food quality (whole vs. processed), portion control, eating patterns (intermittent fasting, etc.)
2. **Hydration** — water intake, electrolyte balance, caffeine/alcohol effects on hydration
3. **Supplementation** — vitamins, minerals, protein, omega-3s, creatine, etc. (only where diet has gaps)
4. **Substance use** — alcohol, nicotine, recreational drugs, caffeine moderation
5. **Gut health** — microbiome, fermented foods, prebiotics/probiotics, digestion

## Physical Body — Movement

6. **Cardiovascular training** — Zone 2 (low-intensity steady state), HIIT, VO2 max work
7. **Strength training** — resistance/hypertrophy, progressive overload, compound lifts
8. **Mobility & flexibility** — stretching, yoga, joint range of motion
9. **Stability & balance** — core work, proprioception, fall prevention (more important with age)
10. **Daily activity (NEAT)** — step count, standing time, taking stairs — non-exercise movement
11. **Skill-based movement** — sports, dance, martial arts (engagement + coordination)

## Physical Body — Recovery & Maintenance

12. **Sleep** — duration (7–9 hrs), quality, sleep hygiene, consistent schedule, circadian rhythm
13. **Recovery** — rest days, deload weeks, soft tissue work (foam rolling, massage), sauna/cold exposure
14. **Posture & ergonomics** — desk setup, sitting habits, screen positioning
15. **Preventive healthcare** — annual physicals, blood panels, dental/vision, age-appropriate screenings (colonoscopy, mammogram, etc.), vaccinations
16. **Hormonal health** — thyroid, sex hormones, cortisol, insulin sensitivity
17. **Skin, oral, hearing, eye health** — often overlooked but compound over decades

## Mental Health

18. **Stress management** — identifying stressors, coping techniques, nervous system regulation
19. **Emotional regulation** — recognizing, naming, and processing emotions
20. **Mindfulness & meditation** — attention training, present-moment awareness
21. **Therapy / professional support** — counseling, CBT, medication where appropriate
22. **Self-talk & mindset** — internal narrative, self-compassion, reframing
23. **Cognitive health** — learning, focus, memory, mental challenge (puzzles, reading, new skills)
24. **Purpose & meaning** — values clarity, goals beyond yourself, sense of contribution

## Social & Relational

25. **Close relationships** — family, partner, deep friendships (strongest predictor of longevity in many studies)
26. **Community & belonging** — clubs, religious/spiritual groups, regular social rhythms
27. **Communication skills** — conflict resolution, assertiveness, listening
28. **Boundaries** — saying no, protecting time and energy
29. **Loneliness mitigation** — proactive social contact, especially as you age

## Behavioral / Lifestyle Systems

30. **Habit formation** — cue-routine-reward design, environment design, stacking
31. **Time management** — scheduling health behaviors, not relying on willpower
32. **Goal setting & tracking** — measurable targets, progress monitoring (weight, lifts, biometrics)
33. **Self-monitoring** — wearables (HRV, steps, sleep), journaling, body measurements
34. **Digital wellbeing** — screen time, notification hygiene, social media boundaries

## Environment

35. **Sunlight & nature** — morning light for circadian rhythm, time outdoors, "green exercise"
36. **Air quality** — indoor air, ventilation, avoiding pollution where possible
37. **Living space** — clutter, noise, lighting, temperature for sleep
38. **Food environment** — what's in your kitchen determines what you eat
39. **Social environment** — the people around you shape your norms (Roseto effect, etc.)

## Often-Missed Segments

40. **Pelvic floor & breathwork** — diaphragmatic breathing, nasal breathing, Valsalva mechanics
41. **Sexual health** — function, intimacy, STI prevention, hormonal aspects
42. **Financial health** — chronic financial stress directly impacts physical and mental health
43. **Play & leisure** — hobbies for their own sake, not productivity
44. **Spirituality / contemplative practice** — separate from religion; meaning-making rituals
45. **Death awareness / legacy** — sounds heavy, but research shows it improves life prioritization

---

## The 80/20 Starting Point

If you want to start with the highest-leverage segments:

- Sleep
- Protein + vegetables
- Strength training 2–3×/week
- Zone 2 cardio
- Daily walks
- One close relationship invested in weekly
- A stress-management practice

These seven cover most of the variance. The rest is optimization.
